What the calculator does

It multiplies each possible grade's sold price by its chance, adds them up, subtracts the grading fee and shipping, and compares the result with the raw price. It recommends grading only when the expected gain is at least $25 and at least 20% of the raw price, because grading takes months and a bad grade is a real risk. See the methodology for details.
