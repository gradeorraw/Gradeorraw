Privacy

Effective date: October 25, 2026. Last updated: October 25, 2026.

The short version. This site collects no personal information. It sets no cookies and has no analytics, ads, trackers, forms, logins or email list. Our web host, GitHub, logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Sites we link to (X, eBay, TCGplayer, Ko-fi, Stripe and others) have their own privacy policies.

What this site collects

Nothing, from us. We don't run cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, fingerprinting, contact forms, comments, accounts or newsletters. We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any from the site.

  • Calculator: runs entirely in your browser. Numbers you type are not sent to us or stored. If you use "Copy share link", your numbers are put into the page address (URL) so you can share it; anyone you send that link to can see them.
  • Verdicts: the page loads a public data file (verdicts.json) from the same site. Nothing about you is sent.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. We don't receive or use those logs. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Sites we link to

When you click a link to another site, that site's privacy policy applies. Affiliate (paid) links go through the affiliate network so the store can credit the referral; the network and store may set cookies or record the click and any purchase. We may receive reports of clicks and commissions, which don't identify you to us by name.

  • X: X Privacy Policy. Posts you make tagging @GradeOrRaw are public on X.
  • eBay and the eBay Partner Network: eBay User Privacy Notice.
  • TCGplayer and impact.com (TCGplayer's affiliate network): TCGplayer Privacy Policy and impact.com Privacy Policy.
  • Ko-fi (tips): Ko-fi Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe (paid checks, when offered): Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • PSA and other graders: see their own sites.

If you contact us or pay us

If you message or tag us on X, we see what X shows us (your username, profile and post). We use it only to answer you. If you tip through Ko-fi or, when offered, buy a paid check through Stripe, the payment provider processes your payment. We receive the details needed to deliver what you paid for and keep records, such as your name, email address, the amount and the date, but never your full card number. Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. X requires users to be at least 13.

Your choices and California notes

Because the site collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or opt you out of. The site doesn't track you over time or across other sites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything here. If you contacted us on X or paid us and want us to delete what we have, ask us; we'll delete it unless we must keep it (for example tax records).

Changes

If we ever add analytics, an email list, comments or on-site payments, we'll update this page before turning them on and change the date at the top.
