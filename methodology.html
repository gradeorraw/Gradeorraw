Methodology

Two steps: today's expected value, then a forward outlook. Both are shown in every verdict.

Step 1: today's expected value

EV(grade) = P10 × V10 + P9 × V9 + P≤8 × V≤8 − (fee + ship in + ship back + insurance)
Grading gain = EV(grade) − V(raw)
Ship back per card = PSA return fee for the order ÷ cards in the order

Where each input comes from
InputWhere it comes from
V(raw), V9, V10Median of the last 5 to 10 sold comps (sold, not asking). Fewer than 5 comps is flagged as low confidence.
V≤8PSA 8 comps, or the raw value if there are no PSA 8 sales (a low grade rarely beats raw).
P10, P9Start from the card's PSA gem rate (PSA 10 pop ÷ total pop), then adjust down for visible flaws in the photo: centering, whitening, scratches.
P≤81 − P10 − P9.
FeeCheapest open tier whose max declared value covers the card if it hits a 10.
ShippingPSA return fee from its postage table, split across the cards in the order, plus inbound postage and insurance.

Gem rates come from cards people chose to submit, so they run high for an average raw copy. That's one more reason we shade P10 down.

The verdict rule

Today's gainVerdict
At least $25 and at least 20% of raw valueGrade
Between $0 and that barBorderline grade only for your own collection
$0 or lessKeep raw

The $25 / 20% bar is a starting choice, not a researched constant. It covers months of turnaround and the risk of a bad grade. We'll tune it as verdicts build up and say so when it changes.

Step 2: the outlook (grade now, grade later, keep raw)

Each factor is scored 0 to 10, weighted, and summed into a 0–100 projection score.

FactorWeightHow it's scored
Supply / print run25%No official print runs exist. Proxies: era, rarity tier (SIR, IR, hyper rare), total PSA pop, and whether a reprint is announced.
Out of print, and for how long20%Months since sealed product was last at MSRP in big retail. Over 24 months scores high. Rotation alone doesn't count.
Pokémon popularity20%Fan-favorite tier (Charizard, Pikachu, Eeveelutions, Gengar and so on) plus recent mention volume.
Set popularity15%Sealed price vs MSRP, number of chase cards, and how much collectors talk about the set.
Price momentum10%90-day trend in raw and PSA 10 sold medians.
PSA 10 pop growth10%Fast growth in PSA 10 pop means more supply coming, so it scores low.
Today's gainProjection scoreCall
Passes the barAnyGrade now
Below the bar65 or moreGrade later re-check in about 90 days, or when a cheaper tier opens
Below the barUnder 65Keep raw

The weights and the 65 cutoff are a first guess. The outlook is an opinion based on public signals, not a forecast. The Pokémon Company doesn't publish per-set or per-card print runs or out-of-print dates, and leaving Standard rotation doesn't mean printing stopped, so supply and out-of-print scores are always labeled as estimates.

Where the numbers come from

  • eBay sold listings for raw and graded sold prices, looked up by hand (no scraping).
  • PSA Population Report for grade counts and gem rates.
  • PSA Auction Prices Realized for graded sale history.
  • TCGplayer market prices as a cross-check on raw value.

House rules

  • Every verdict is dated and shows its comp count.
  • Any number we couldn't source is labeled "est." or "unknown", never filled with a guess presented as fact.
  • Paid links are labeled right next to the link.
  • Not financial advice: we show the math so you can decide.
