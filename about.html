About & disclosure

About Grade or Raw

Grade or Raw is a free, X-first service that tells Pokémon TCG collectors whether a card is worth grading now, later, or not at all. It's for people who pulled a chase card and wonder if it's worth $60+ to slab, returning collectors with old binders, and small sellers who want quick math before a submission.

Verdicts are researched with AI assistance and posted by a human. Every verdict shows its sources and math so you don't have to take our word for it.

Contact: @GradeOrRaw on X.

Affiliate disclosure (paid links)

We earn a commission from some links. Some links on this site and in our posts on X are paid affiliate links, for example to eBay (through the eBay Partner Network) or TCGplayer. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Paid links are labeled "paid link" right next to the link, in the post or entry itself. Commissions never change a verdict. We say "keep raw" whenever the math says so, and we don't get paid by grading companies.

Not financial advice

Verdicts and the calculator are for information and entertainment. They're opinions based on public sales data and stated assumptions. Prices and grading fees change. Check current sold listings and the grader's own price list before you spend money.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or trackers. The calculator runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log basic request data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
